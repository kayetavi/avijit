📌 Engineering Basis & Standards Applicability:

  • API 574 (Piping Inspection) Section 11 & API 581 (RBI Methodology) Annex D: Structural minimum thickness ($t_{\text{struct}}$) governs over pressure design thickness for smaller pipe sizes or low-pressure systems due to mechanical span loads, self-weight, vibration, and handling.
  • ASME B36.10M / B36.19M & ASTM Mill Under-Tolerance: Standard commercial pipe manufacturing allows up to 12.5% under-nominal wall thickness tolerance. Design specification must ensure nominal pipe thickness satisfies: $t_{\text{nom}} \ge \frac{t_{\text{struct}}}{1 - \text{Mill\_Tol}} + \text{CA}$.
  • ASME B16.5 Flange Ratings: Classes 150, 300, 600, 900, 1500, and 2500 correspond to pressure-temperature rating classes governing the pipe connection integrity.
