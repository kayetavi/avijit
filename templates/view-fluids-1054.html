Engineering Nomenclature & Definitions (API 581 Table 4.1)

Thermophysical parameter references used in Level 2 consequence of failure modeling

Standard Reference
MW Molecular Weight (kg/kmol or g/mol) — Critical parameter for vapor density, dispersion rate, and flash fraction calculations.
NBP Normal Boiling Point (°C / °F) — Boiling temperature at 1.013 bar absolute pressure, determining phase flashing behavior upon atmospheric release.
AIT Auto-Ignition Temperature (°C / °F) — Lowest temperature at which the chemical vapor spontaneously ignites in normal atmosphere without an external flame.
Streams Representative Process Materials — Corresponding hydrocarbon blends, refinery cuts, petrochemical intermediates, and service metallurgy.
